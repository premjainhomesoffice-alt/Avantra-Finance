How It Works

A process built to
close, not stall.

Most deals fall apart during the conditional period — not because of the deal, but because the team is siloed. Avantra eliminates that by owning every step.

Initial Consultation — 30 Minutes, No Commitment

Document Collection — Precise, Not Excessive
A targeted checklist based on your specific profile — T4 or T1/NOA for self-employed, bank statements, CRA documents for CSBFL. Only what your file and target program actually need.
